# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Discuss technical decisions on their
merits, welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels, and give
credit for others' work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sustained
disruption, doxxing, and publication of private security information are not
acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project repositories and in public spaces where a
person represents Bedriox or Veno Ninja LLC. Report conduct concerns privately
to the Veno Ninja LLC repository administrators. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, limit participation, or ban participants when needed to protect the
community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
